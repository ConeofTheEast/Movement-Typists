    Plane & axis

    The sagittal plane divides the body into left and right. You are looking at it side-on. Its axis, the frontal axis ⊙, pokes straight out of the screen through every joint.

    Only flexion (joint angle decreases) and extension (joint angle increases) happen here. Abduction, adduction and rotation belong to other planes, and the corridor will reject them.

    Contractions

    CConcentric: the muscle shortens under tension.

    EEccentric: the muscle lengthens under tension (braking).

    IIsometric: tension with no change in length.

    Muscles swell and glow in these colours as the android moves.
